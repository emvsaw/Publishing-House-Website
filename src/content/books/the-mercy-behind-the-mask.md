He was hired to guard the syndicate's darkest secrets. She's determined to strip him of every last one—starting with his control.

Mina March knows the signature that stole her inheritance is a forgery. She knows because as a girl, she used to forge her father's handwriting herself. Armed with financial evidence, she's crashing Saturday's masked gala to force the Valtier family to account for every stolen cent.

There's only one obstacle in her way: Cassian Dane, the syndicate's lethal head of security.

Cassian has strict orders to escort Mina safely to the gala and seize her notes before she can file them with prosecutors. Mina has seen what Cassian's hands can do—she watched him break an attacker's wrist without breaking a sweat. But instead of fear, the dangerous friction between them ignites an explosive, forbidden craving. She wants those lethal hands on her body, and she's bold enough to dictate exactly how he gets to touch her.

But Cassian carries a secret far more lethal than the financial ledger. He was on duty the night Mina's father died, and he knows who locked the door that kept her from his bedside. Handing Mina the truth will give her the weapons to bring down the syndicate—and ruin Cassian right alongside them.

*The Mercy Behind the Mask* is a high-heat, dark organized-crime romance featuring a disinherited heiress, her ruthlessly protective bodyguard, forbidden touch, high steam (5/5), and a guaranteed HEA.
