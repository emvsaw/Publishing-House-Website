**She came to Spain for a fresh start. The village supplied a body.**

Newly divorced at fifty-two, Goya Winters Sánchez is still learning to run her late aunt's seaside guesthouse when the summer festival ends in murder. A commissioner collapses after the ceremonial toast, and suspicion falls on the baker who poured it: her cook Lola's cousin.

Goya knows a convincing story can still be false. To find out who is rewriting this one, she'll need Daniel, a quiet former vet who knows which villagers will talk. But the clues are leading closer to home, and some truths are harder to share than others.

- A fresh start at fifty-two in a Spanish seaside village
- Opposites attract in a slow-burn, later-in-life romance (clean and closed-door)
- Reluctant sleuthing partners and a fairly clued festival whodunit
- Found family around Lola's kitchen table
- Village secrets, gossip, and dry humor
