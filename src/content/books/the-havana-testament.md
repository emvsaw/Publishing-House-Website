**Rum, ruined palaces, and a fortune that was never theirs to sell.**

Havana, 1926. A grand old sugar dynasty is selling its treasures, and Toni von Treuenfels has been hired to put her signature on every piece. The paintings are genuine. The silver is genuine. The papers that say the family owns them are something else entirely.

The estate manager who knew where everything came from is dead, and the room where he died has been arranged a little too neatly. The family has secrets in every courtyard, and someone with friends in the presidential palace wants them kept.

Toni has kept love light for years. In Havana, a reckless young journalist makes it clear she wants her. And at the Gran Teatro, the pianist Toni has never stopped thinking about walks back into her life. This time, she will have to choose.

**Inside:** Prohibition-era glamour · family secrets · murder · a love triangle · found family abroad · the slow burn, finally burning

*The Havana Testament* is Book 3 of The Authenticator series. Start reading today.
