He rules the city's darkest syndicate. She holds the evidence to burn it to the ground. But trapped in his compound, burning him down is the last thing on her mind.

*A dark mafia romance: enemies to allies, forced proximity, morally grey hero.*

Forensic accountant Seraphina Sorrell only meant to trace an eleven-cent discrepancy. She didn't expect to lose her key witness—or find Nico Valtier standing in her hallway.

Cold, ruthless, and unnervingly protective, Nico offers her sanctuary. Seraphina meets him with a kitchen knife and sets brutal terms: a locked room under his roof, total control of her evidence, and a dead-man's switch with prosecutors if she misses a check-in.

Nico accepts every condition without blinking. She thought she'd won the negotiation. She should have asked why he made it so easy.

Isolated inside his sprawling compound, the boundary between enemy and protector blurs into intense, relentless temptation. Nico steps between her and every danger—and every touch makes it harder to remember why she kept her door locked.

But when Seraphina uncovers a hidden record dated before she ever set foot in his club, she realizes Nico didn't just find her by chance. He bargained for her long before she ever made her terms.

*A Bargain Written in Ash* is a standalone dark mafia romance featuring an obsessive, touch-her-and-die hero, intense forced proximity, high-heat open-door encounters, and a guaranteed HEA.
