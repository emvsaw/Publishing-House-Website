**The wine is breathing. One of the guests isn't.**

Autumn in Calablanca brings excellent food, cooler evenings, and a welcome opportunity for Goya Winters Sánchez to enjoy a village event without investigating anyone.

An ambitious plan, as it turns out.

When Jaime Osuna is found murdered during Bodega del Mar's food-and-wine weekend, the people who knew him best have remarkably little good to say about him. His estranged son has plenty to explain. The kitchen has grievances of its own. And beneath the polite smiles, old resentments are coming to the boil.

Goya would prefer to concentrate on her guesthouse—and the rather distracting Daniel. Instead, she finds herself untangling family loyalties and alibis that sound considerably better than they hold up.

Meanwhile, a discovery among her aunt's papers could change everything for someone Goya loves.

In a village where protecting your own is practically a civic duty, telling a harmless secret from a murderous lie won't be easy. Especially when everyone insists they're only trying to help.

*A Spanish culinary cozy with sharp-eyed sleuthing, complicated families, and romance worth lingering over.*
