**The wine is breathing. The host isn't.**

Goya and Daniel only wanted one quiet weekend at Bodega del Mar, without the whole village watching. Then the host is found dead, his family's loyalties start to crack, and every alibi sounds better than it holds up.

Worse, what Goya and Daniel saw while they were supposed to be alone could change everything, if they're ready for Calablanca to know about them.

And among her late aunt's papers, Goya finds a secret that isn't hers to tell.

- A later-in-life romance they're trying to keep quiet (clean and closed-door)
- Opposites attract, now learning to be a couple
- A food-and-wine weekend whodunit at a seaside bodega
- Found family, divided loyalties, and dry humor
