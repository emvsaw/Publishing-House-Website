Seven years ago, he put a price on her heart. Now, he's offering her a ring—and she's rewriting every word of the contract.

*A dark mafia arranged marriage romance: rival heirs, enemies to lovers, negotiated marriage.*

Rhea Vickers has until Friday to call in the debts of 312 employees or lose her rightful claim to the Baines empire. When her ruthless ex-lover and rival heir, Damian Cross, walks into her office offering a strategic marriage of convenience, Rhea knows better than to trust him. Marrying Damian gives her the votes she needs to fight back, but she isn't taking any chances: she takes a red pen to his forty-one-page prenuptial agreement, establishing a strict exit clause and keeping her fortune entirely her own.

Damian agrees to every line. He thinks he's playing a long game. Rhea knows she's walking into the lion's den.

She remembers the cold-blooded betrayal that tore them apart seven years ago. But she also remembers exactly how Damian feels against her, and precisely where to kiss him to make him lose all train of thought. Their wedding night proves dangerously explosive—a fiery collision of lingering grief, dark possessiveness, and unquenched desire that leaves zero doubt about how deeply they still consume each other.

But as the syndicate's shadows deepen, Rhea uncovers the real names behind the debts she's forced to collect. To keep the power she married for, she'll have to destroy innocent lives. To walk away, she needs the dark truth about what Damian did seven years ago—and Damian will have to face the incriminating files Rhea's been keeping on him ever since.

*A Crown of Unpaid Promises* is an explosive, high-heat dark organized-crime romance featuring a marriage of convenience, second-chance angst, rival heirs to lovers, 5/5 steam, and a guaranteed HEA that concludes The Velvet Reckoning trilogy.
